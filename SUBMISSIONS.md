## 📌 HOW TO USE THIS PAGE

This is a real newspaper page, already laid out and half written. The editors have filled some stories in. The rest are marked **SLOT** and are waiting for you.

1. Pick **one** empty slot below. Only one per pull request.
2. Replace the placeholder text with your own story. Keep the headline, the byline, and the paragraph shape.
3. Delete the `CLAIM THIS SLOT` comment line above your story so the editors know it is taken.
4. Run `python3 scripts/proofread.py` until it prints the line `🎉 Everything is fit to print`.
5. Commit, push to your fork, and open a pull request.

**House rules.** Every story is fiction. Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. Quotation marks come in pairs. Two or three short paragraphs is a full story. If you invent a person, give them a name and a job.

### LAST TRAIN OF THE NIGHT ARRIVES EARLY, NOBODY KNOWS WHAT TO DO

*By The Night Editor, City Desk*

The 23:47 service to Harbourside pulled into Platform 4 at 23:44 on Saturday, three minutes ahead of schedule, causing what one commuter described as "a small crisis of faith" among the forty people waiting on the platform.

"I have caught this train every night for nine years," said Bernadette Cole, 51, a hospital porter. "It has never once been early. I checked the board twice. I checked my watch. Then I got on, because what else can you do?"

Transit officials confirmed the early arrival and said an investigation was under way. The driver, reached by telephone, said the track was clear and he "simply kept going."
