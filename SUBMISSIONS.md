### PIGEONS UNIONISE, DEMAND SHORTER STATUE SHIFTS

*By The Editor, Example Desk*

The city's pigeons announced on Saturday that they had formed a union and would no longer sit on the statue of General Ashford for more than four hours at a stretch. A spokesbird, perched on a lamppost outside City Hall, said the current arrangement was "frankly exhausting" and that the general "does not even have a hat."

City officials said they were reviewing the demands and had ordered a hat.
