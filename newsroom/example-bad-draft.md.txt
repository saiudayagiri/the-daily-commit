### LOCAL CAT ELECTED TO SCHOOL BOARD , PROMISES MORE NAPS

*By A. Student, Newsroom*

in a stunning upset on saturday, a ginger cat named Biscuit won a seat on the the district school board with 61 percent of the vote.Voters said they were tired of long meetings and Biscuit has promised to keep every session under ten minutes

"i think its a good sign for democracy" said one voter , who asked not to be named. Biscuit's campaign manager , a nine year old named Priya, said the cat would definately focus on longer lunch breaks and a extra recess.

Critics pointed out that Biscuit is a cat. Supporters replied that your going to have to accept it. The board's first meeting is on monday, and Biscuit is expected to sleep through most of it
